Why CakeNest exists

We began with one belief. A home baker is a business waiting to be believed in.

CakeNest started as a community of people who loved to bake. Over the years we saw the same story again and again. Gifted bakers, working from their own kitchens, had the skill but not the support. They weren't sure how to price a cake, how to get noticed, or how to turn a passion into an income.

So we built that support, one piece at a time. Classes to learn from. A community that cheers you on. Social media that brings customers to your door. Tools that run the business side. And when a baker is ready for a studio or a café, a team that builds the brand with them.

Today thousands of home bakers have joined us. Many now earn their living from baking. Some have their own studios with their names above the door. That is what we mean by a bakepreneur.

Our mission To help every talented home baker in India build a brand that customers remember, trust and order from.
