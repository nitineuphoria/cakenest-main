We didn't arrive at home baking. We grew up in it. Seven years, one obsession: making the home baker's life bigger.

You're not 'just' a home baker.

You wake up before the house does. You answer messages with flour on your hands. You remember every customer's 'less sweet, she's diabetic' and every kid's favourite cartoon. You've built something real from a corner of your kitchen — and most of the world still calls it a hobby.

We never did.

We saw what you actually are: a business owner, an artist, a one-woman operation doing the work of five people. We also saw what was missing — nobody was building for you. The apps were too corporate. The advice was too generic. The big platforms wanted your orders but never your growth.

So we built the opposite. A home for the whole journey. The place that remembers your deliveries so you don't lose one, shows you which cakes actually make money, teaches you what nobody taught us, and puts your work in front of the customers who've been looking for exactly you.

This is what we wish someone had handed us on day one.

Everything a home baker needs. In one place. For the first time.

Most bakers stitch together ten tools and still feel alone. CakeNest is the one ecosystem built end to end — learn it, run it, sell it, grow it.
