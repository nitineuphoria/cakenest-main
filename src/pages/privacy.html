3. Your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit a form or contact us. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to {{legal:email}}. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it, but we may no longer be able to help with your enquiry or application.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run this website and only for the purposes above:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage of careers applications and CVs, security and bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile), and privacy-friendly visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies).
  • Resend: sends us an email alert when a careers application arrives.
  • Google: provides the fonts on this website (your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address), and hosts the inbox where our team reads application alerts.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you choose to message us on WhatsApp.

We may also disclose personal data if the law requires it. Some of these providers may process data outside India, with safeguards in line with the DPDP Act.

5. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies to protect the site from bots and abuse.

6. How long we keep it

  • Careers applications and CVs: up to 12 months after you apply, then deleted automatically. If you're selected, we keep your application with your employment records.
  • Join and Partner enquiries and other messages: while we're in touch, and for no more than 24 months after our last conversation, unless you become a client or partner.

You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time (see section 7).

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update your personal data;
  • have your personal data erased;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • have your grievances addressed; and
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to.

To use any of these rights, write to {{legal:email}}. We'll respond as soon as we can and within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must fill in our forms for you or with you, and by doing so they give consent on your behalf. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children without a parent's or guardian's consent; if we find we have, we'll delete it. Careers applications are open only to people aged 18 and over.

9. How we protect it

The website runs only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Careers applications and CVs are stored in restricted storage, and only authorised CakeNest team members can open them, after signing in with a one-time code sent to an approved email address and a password. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will act promptly if anything goes wrong, including notifying you and the authorities where the law requires it.
