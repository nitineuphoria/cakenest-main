Who can join CakeNest?{{i:plus}}
Anyone who bakes, or wants to learn. Most of our community are women working from home, and we've worked with bakers from 14 to 55.

      
Do I need a shop or a big investment?{{i:plus}}
No. Most of our bakers started in their own home kitchens. We help you build from where you are.

      
I've never sold a cake. Where do I start?{{i:plus}}
Start by learning. Our live online courses cover the craft and the basics of pricing, so you're ready for your first order.

      
How will customers find me?{{i:plus}}
Through a strong social presence built with CakeNest Marketing, and through the CakeNest marketplace where customers order cakes online.

      
What happens after I send the form?{{i:plus}}
Our team reaches out on WhatsApp to understand your goals and suggest the right first step.
